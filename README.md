🚲Bike Sales Analysis Project

---

Welcome to the Bike Sales Data Analysis Project! This project explores and analyzes customer demographics and behavioral traits to uncover the key factors that drive bicycle purchases.

📊 Project Overview

---

This project includes:
*  **Cleaning and preparing** raw customer demographic records.
*  **Analyzing purchase trends** across income levels, age brackets, and geographic regions.
*  **Visualizing customer behavior** to identify core purchasing segments and commute limitations.
*  **Providing actionable insights** for targeted marketing, product positioning, and inventory allocation.

📈 Key Insights

---

Here are the primary trends uncovered from the data:
*  💵 **Income Thresholds**: Higher average income levels strongly correlate with bike purchases across both genders, peaking with males earning over $60k.
*  🎂 **Prime Target Demographics**: Middle-aged individuals represent the vast majority of the customer base and total purchase volume.
*  🚲 **Commute Boundaries**: Bike purchasing peaks heavily for individuals with short commutes (0–1 miles) and drops drastically once commutes exceed 2–5 miles.
